# Namaste Video design directions

Three homepage concepts are under review. The product remains an invite-only tool for turning a topic into a narrated vertical video. Keep the home experience approachable for someone who has never edited a film.

## Reference styles

The live NamasteDev dark homepage uses a system sans-serif stack for its hero, body and primary action. Use the same stack in Namaste Video. The exact typeface displayed depends on the operating system.

```css
font-family: ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, "Noto Sans", sans-serif, "Apple Color Emoji", "Segoe UI Emoji", "Segoe UI Symbol", "Noto Color Emoji";
```

Use bold 700 for headlines and 400 for paragraphs. Set headings in warm white #F7F5F0 and supporting text in #A4A4A6 against black. Highlight a short phrase with a 110-degree gradient through #FFEDB0, #FFC36C and #EB923D. Use an orange pill for the main action, grading #E58C33 to #D78330 with #241507 lettering. Keep instructional text solid. Give keyboard focus its own visible outline.

## Homepage concepts

| Concept | What the visitor experiences | Design question |
|---|---|---|
| Watch the lesson | A split hero pairs a concrete question with an illustrated water-cycle film. A short scene strip invites closer inspection. | Can a first-time visitor understand the output by watching one example? |
| Start with a question | A large notes field opens with a familiar question about rain. Directly below, three scenes show what a plan could look like. | Does seeing a starting point make entering a topic feel easier? |
| Inside the storyboard | A selected illustration sits beside its narration. A timed visual cue shows how a change in the script affects the scene. | Can creators see how much editorial control they have? |

## Shared interaction

Use original illustrations and educational copy. Keep the mobile reading order heading, action, artwork, supporting content. Give motion a deliberate start and a visible pause control. Under reduced motion, show a static key frame until playback is requested. Reserve audio for deliberate playback. Keep the design-review navigation separate from product navigation.
